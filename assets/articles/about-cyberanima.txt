This website is the main digital space where most of (if not all) the work performed by the CyberAnima Association and the results thereof will be displayed. It is also where thematic, narrative, literary and/or otherwise written explorations taken by the Association may be published. A more precise, descriptive and extensive, albeit, perhaps, not exhaustive, presentation of the motivation behind this work is found under the "Why" section.

Additionally, software developed by the Association will also be mentioned in the "How" section, where Projects of various natures will be listed along their related links and relevant information attached.

As our work has the tendency of being dispersed throughout different channels of communication and platforms of digital content distribution, mentions and links to other such places can be found in the "Where" section.

Finally, the "Who" section contains a list of our current members alongside a brief description of what they may consider as their main "office" or regular activity. Expect changes to this section as its content accommodates the wishes of each concerned party (which is especially relevant for this section, but also valid for the other ones).
